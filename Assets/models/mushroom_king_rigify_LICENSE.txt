Mushroom King Rigify game asset

Source model: user-supplied models/mushroom_king.fbx (superseded original removed
during unused-asset cleanup; active mushroom_king_rigify FBX sources are retained).
Only the MushroomKing body is used; the separate Mushroom weapon is excluded.
Body geometry and UVs preserved: 2,835 vertices, 5,484 triangles.
Fitted Rigify metarig with automatic weights and preserved rigid head details.
Export weights are normalized and limited to four influences per vertex.

Animation: Quaternius Universal Animation Library (UAL), CC0 1.0.
Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html
Same source clips as the basic Alien enemy:
walk: Walk_Fwd_Loop, frames 0-40, 30 FPS, in-place.
melee: Punch_Cross, frames 0-30, 30 FPS.
hit: Hit_Chest, frames 0-10, 30 FPS.
Motion is adapted through fitted Rigify controls; gameplay owns world movement.
All 58 export bones, including the coordinate-conversion root, are retained.
Body and clip FBXs bake the same 0.2 scale; engine import scale is 1.

Texture: Quaternius Ultimate Monsters atlas, CC0 1.0.
Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemonsters.html
Material: materials/quaternius_monsters_atlas.vmat.

Editable Mushroom_Rigify.blend and Mushroom_Rigify_Animated.blend authoring
sources retain the metarig, control rig, drivers, packed atlas and full weights.
