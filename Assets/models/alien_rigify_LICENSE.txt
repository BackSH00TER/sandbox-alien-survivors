Alien Rigify game asset

Source model: user-supplied models/alien.fbx (superseded original removed during
unused-asset cleanup; the active alien_rigify FBX build sources are retained).
Original geometry and UVs are preserved: 3,873 vertices, 7,676 triangles.
The replacement uses a fitted Rigify rig and normalized, at most four
deform-bone weights per vertex. Rigid head details retain their head binding.

Animation: Quaternius Universal Animation Library (UAL), CC0 1.0.
Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html
walk: Walk_Fwd_Loop, frames 0-40, 30 FPS, retargeted to Alien_Rig controls.
The model imports frames 0-40, retaining the matching interpolation endpoint.
The walk is in-place; gameplay owns world movement. Horizontal distance traveled
controls playback, including on remote peers, so pursuit surges and slows also
speed up and slow down the gait. The current walk pose is held
while stationary or descending; no additional idle clip is supplied.
Pursuit uses staggered, 0.65-second surge/recovery steps with 30% planted recovery.
PursuitStepDuration and PursuitStepRecoveryFraction tune this cadence for all
enemy variants; MoveSpeed/RangedMoveSpeed cap peak speed, not average speed.
The default 100-unit peak yields roughly 35 units/second and 23 units per step.
Recovery never boosts surge speed to compensate for the pause. Crowd separation
shares the cadence, while committed melee lunges retain their existing timing.

melee: Punch_Cross, frames 0-30, 30 FPS, non-looping.
hit: Hit_Chest, frames 0-10, 30 FPS, non-looping.
Both are retargeted to the same Alien Rigify controls and deform skeleton.
The melee clip is timed to the existing windup, lunge and recovery. Its maximum
right-hand forward reach (source frame 9 of 30) coincides with the existing
damage instant; animation never moves the actor or applies damage.
Visual recovery has a minimum 0.08-second fade even if combat recovery is zero.
Hit flinches take visual priority, then return to the still-advancing punch or
walk. Repeated hits do not restart an already playing flinch. HitStaggerDuration
still decides whether damage actually interrupts combat; its zero default
does not gain hitstun. Host-only broadcasts trigger both one-shots on all peers.
Runtime uses a three-clip AnimGraph2 blend, with independently timed attack and
hit-reaction weights. ModelDoc sequences remain available for asset preview.

Texture: Quaternius Ultimate Monsters atlas, CC0 1.0.
Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemonsters.html
Material: materials/quaternius_monsters_atlas.vmat, shared without modification.

Both body and walk FBXs bake the same 0.2 scale; ModelDoc import scale is 1.
ModelDoc retains all 58 skeleton bones with BoneMarkupList bone_cull_type None.
The unweighted root is required: removing it loses the native graph's root
orientation and turns rendered locomotion/punches 90 degrees from actor facing.
The visual child remains unrotated; gameplay facing and hitboxes are unchanged.
The editable Alien_Rigify.blend, Alien_Rigify_Walk.blend and
Alien_Rigify_Combat.blend authoring sources
retain the metarig, control rig, drivers, packed atlas and unpruned weights.
